And so, it begins.

With my heart a veritable kaleidoscope of emotions. Hope, excitement, a touch of trepidation, and an overwhelming sense of purpose.

I have been preparing for this trip for months. Not just logistically — the permits, the equipment, the contacts on the ground — but emotionally. Because every time I step into a wild place, I am reminded of how small I am, and how much is at stake.

The elephants were the first thing I saw. A matriarch leading her family across a dry riverbed at dusk, the light catching the dust around their feet like a kind of gold. I didn't raise my camera immediately. I just watched. I let the moment be a moment before I let it become a photograph.

That is the discipline I keep returning to. The pause before the shutter. The breath before the frame. Because the best images I have ever made were not the ones I rushed towards — they were the ones I waited for.

This journey is just beginning. There is so much still to see, to document, to witness. And I am grateful — deeply, genuinely grateful — to be here for it.
