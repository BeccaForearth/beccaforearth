This project took me to the heart of one of the most urgent conservation stories on the planet: the last two northern white rhinos in existence, protected around the clock at Ol Pejeta Conservancy in Kenya.

Over two weeks, I embedded with the ranger teams, documenting their daily routines, the emotional weight of their work, and the extraordinary bond that forms between humans and the animals they protect.

The resulting images were used in an international awareness campaign and featured in a conservation fundraising report that exceeded its target by 40%.
