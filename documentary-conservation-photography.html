Conservation First

Every Image Is an Argument

Long-form documentary work bearing witness to the natural world — and making the case for protecting it.

The Foundation

Conservation photography is where everything begins.

Before I was an ESG storyteller, before I was a sustainability advocate, I was a photographer in the field — watching, waiting, listening. My conservation work is the foundation of everything I do, and it remains the most important work I produce.

I photograph wildlife, habitat, and the people who dedicate their lives to protecting both. I work in the field with the patience and rigour that documentary photography demands — and I bring the same standards of authenticity and minimal intervention that define my ESG work. What you see in my images is what was there. No staging. No compositing. No manufactured drama.

This work matters in its own right. It also matters because it is the source of my credibility as an ESG photographer. I understand the natural systems that your environmental commitments are meant to serve — because I have spent years documenting them. That context makes every image I take for a corporate client more powerful, more specific, and more true.

My conservation photography is available for editorial use, exhibition, and licensing. I also work with conservation organisations, NGOs, and research institutions to document their work in the field — creating visual evidence of impact that serves both scientific and communications purposes.
