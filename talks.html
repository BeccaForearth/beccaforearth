The Speaker

I don't just speak about the natural world. I make you feel responsible for it.

I speak at conferences, corporate events, sustainability summits, and academic institutions about the power of authentic visual storytelling in the age of ESG accountability. My talks are built on years of field experience, academic rigour, and a deep belief that the way we tell stories about our impact matters as much as the impact itself.

My background spans documentary photography, conservation fieldwork, and a Master's in Education for Sustainability. I bring all of it to the stage — and I leave audiences not just informed, but moved. The kind of moved that leads to different decisions.

I am available for keynote addresses, panel discussions, workshop facilitation, and bespoke corporate events. I work with sustainability teams, communications departments, investor relations functions, and leadership teams who want their people to understand why authentic storytelling is the most powerful tool they have.

Proof, Not Promise: The Case for Authentic ESG Photography

A keynote for sustainability and communications professionals on why authentic visual evidence is the most powerful tool in your ESG communications — and what happens when you use stock photography instead.

Open Minds Change Worlds: Photography as Advocacy

A talk about the power of images to change how people think, feel, and act — drawing on field experience from conservation photography and the lessons it holds for corporate communications.

The Greenwashing Gap: Visual Communications in the Age of Accountability

A practical keynote on the regulatory landscape — CSRD, the Green Claims Directive, and what they mean for the images inside your sustainability reports. Designed for legal, compliance, and communications teams.

Bearing Witness: Documentary Photography and the Natural World

A more personal talk — about what it means to spend years photographing wildlife and habitat, and what the natural world teaches us about the urgency of the moment we are living through.
