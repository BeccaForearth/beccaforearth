The Talks

Discover Our Transformative Talks

Each talk explores a unique and distinct theme, inviting us to connect more deeply with our purpose, honour our individual paths, and embrace the shared privilege of caring for one another and the wider world.

My talks are suitable for schools and universities, corporate organisations, festivals, wellness retreats, and community events. Each session can be tailored to your audience and delivered as a keynote, workshop, or panel discussion.

Talk 01

Shifting Perspectives

How photography can change our relationship with nature — and with ourselves. This talk explores the power of the lens as a tool for empathy, awareness, and transformation. Through stunning imagery and personal narrative, Rebecca guides audiences to see the world — and their place in it — differently.

Suitable for: Schools, universities, corporate teams, wellness retreats

Talk 02

The Story Well

The power of narrative in conservation. Every species, ecosystem, and community has a story — and the stories we tell determine what we protect. This talk examines how storytelling shapes conservation outcomes, and how each of us can become a more effective voice for the natural world.

Suitable for: NGOs, environmental organisations, media professionals, educators

Talk 03

The Privilege of Witness

What does it mean to truly see? Drawing from field experiences across four continents, this talk explores the profound responsibility that comes with bearing witness to the world's most vulnerable places and species — and how that responsibility can become a catalyst for personal and collective action.

Suitable for: Conferences, festivals, conservation events, leadership programmes

Talk 04

Wild Curiosity

Reconnecting with the natural world as a source of wonder, creativity, and resilience. This talk is an invitation to rediscover the child who once abscond to the woods — and to understand why that connection is more vital now than ever. Includes practical tools for bringing nature into everyday life and work.

Suitable for: Schools, community groups, mental health organisations, corporate wellness
