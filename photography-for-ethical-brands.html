Brand Photography

Your values are real. Your photography should prove it.

Authentic commercial photography for B Corps, social enterprises and purpose-led businesses.

For Brands That Mean It

The world can tell the difference between a brand that performs its values and one that lives them.

I work with organisations whose ethical commitments are genuine — and whose photography should reflect that. Not polished, not manufactured, not aspirational in the hollow sense. Real. Honest. Specific to who you are and what you actually do.

My background in conservation and sustainability means I understand the natural and social systems your brand is trying to serve. That understanding shows in the images I make. I don't just photograph your products or your people — I photograph the relationship between your work and the world it is trying to improve.

This is not about making things look good. It is about making things look true. In a world where consumers, investors, and regulators are all applying increasing scrutiny to brand claims, the most powerful thing your photography can be is honest.

I work with B Corps, social enterprises, regenerative food and agriculture businesses, sustainable fashion and lifestyle brands, ethical financial services, and any organisation whose values are the foundation of what they do — not a marketing layer on top of it.

Authentic, Not Aspirational

I photograph what is real — your actual operations, your real people, your genuine impact. No stock imagery, no manufactured scenarios, no visual claims you cannot substantiate.

Sustainability Fluent

I understand the language of ESG, B Corp certification, and impact reporting. I know which images your communications need and I shoot with that purpose in mind.

Full Usage Rights

All images delivered with full commercial usage rights — for web, print, social media, annual reports, and investor communications. No licensing complications.

Consistent Visual Identity

For brands with ongoing communications needs, I offer retainer arrangements that build a consistent, growing visual library aligned to your brand identity and values.

Work With Me

If your brand means what it says, let's show the world.

Every engagement begins with a conversation about your brand, your values, and the story you need to tell. I will show you exactly how authentic photography can make your commitments undeniable.
