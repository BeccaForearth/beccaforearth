Who You Are

From the quiet mastery of resourceful artisans and the earth-bound honesty of locally sourced produce, to the mindful attention to slow-stitched fashion and the curators of eco-experiences, you are the brands that prove that living well and doing good are woven from the same thread. You've chosen to put people and planet first; let's make sure your imagery does the same.

What I Do

With sincere warmth and an eco-conscious perspective, I am privileged to create bespoke commercial imagery for ethical brands and sustainably-minded creators. Following an authentic documentary-style approach, I help to tell your brand's story and your intentions — capturing the real people, the careful processes, the genuine experiences and the positive change you foster.

Why It Matters

Building and maintaining trust that promotes environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and the safeguarding of our global heritage for truly sustainable brand growth is vital… for the planet and in my work. Whether your brand is rooted in the commitment to ethical travel and wild adventure or the heritage of slow craftsmanship, let's create visuals that align with your virtues and establish loyalty with your customers.

Creative Fees

We're committed to exceptional commercial photography that captures your brand's essence, with transparent pricing to ensure you understand the value. Our creative fees cover the artistry, expertise, and resources involved in delivering high-quality images tailored to your specific needs. These fees include:

  • Pre-Production Consultation: We collaborate closely with you to understand your vision, goals, and requirements.
  • Photography Session: This encompasses the time spent capturing images, whether on location or in the studio.
  • Post-Production Editing: Each image is meticulously edited to ensure it meets the highest standards of quality and creativity.
  • Delivery of Final Images: You will receive your images in a timely manner, formatted for both digital and print use.

Licensing Fees

Licensing fees are separate from creative fees and are based on how you intend to use the photographs. These fees ensure that you have the appropriate rights to use the images while respecting the photographer's work.

  • Usage Type: Different rates apply depending on whether the images will be used for print, online advertising, social media, or other commercial purposes.
  • Duration of Use: Licensing fees may vary based on how long you plan to use the images.
  • Geographic Scope: The fees may also reflect the geographic areas where the images will be used, from local to international markets.
