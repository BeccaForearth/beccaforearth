The Process

How Wild Collaborations Work

1

Step 1

Eligibility

Tell me about your mission and needs. I prioritise organisations where professional photography is currently a luxury, not a line item.

What I ask: Help me understand your funding reality — whether through a conversation, a funding overview, or an annual report.

2

Step 2

Evaluation

We explore the scope, timing, and context of your project. Together, we assess whether a Wild Collaboration is the right fit — logistically, ethically, and strategically.

What we clarify: Project goals, field conditions, access, timelines, and intended use of imagery.

3

Step 3

Collaboration

I embed within your team to document the work as it unfolds.

What I ask: Simple field accommodation and access to the project environment. Being present allows me to capture the authentic, unscripted moments that define powerful documentary storytelling.

4

Step 4

Impact Delivery

You receive high-resolution imagery ready for grants, campaigns, reports, and advocacy.

What this means: Powerful visual assets designed to attract funding, influence policy, and strengthen public support for your mission.
