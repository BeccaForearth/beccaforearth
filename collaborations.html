Collaborations

I lend my lens to those who lead with heart.

Working with conservation organisations, NGOs, sustainability-focused businesses and media outlets to create authentic visual evidence of impact.

Working Together

The most powerful collaborations happen when values align.

I work with organisations and individuals whose work I believe in — whose commitments are genuine and whose impact is real. I am not a photographer for hire who will shoot anything for anyone. I am a visual storyteller who chooses her collaborators with the same care I bring to every image I make.

If you are doing work that matters — in conservation, in sustainability, in social impact, in ethical business — and you need photography that does justice to that work, I want to hear from you.

I am also open to collaborative projects with other photographers, filmmakers, writers, and researchers working in the space where the natural world meets human responsibility. If you have an idea, let's talk.

Conservation Organisations

Field documentation, impact photography, and visual evidence of conservation outcomes. I work with the patience and rigour that conservation fieldwork demands.

NGOs & Charities

Photography for fundraising campaigns, annual reports, and impact communications. Authentic images that make the case for your work to funders, partners, and the public.

Research Institutions

Documentary photography for academic and scientific projects. Images that serve both publication and public communication purposes.

Media & Editorial

Photography for editorial use in publications covering sustainability, conservation, and environmental affairs. Available for assignment and licensing.

Sustainability Businesses

Collaborative projects with businesses whose sustainability commitments are genuine and whose visual communications should reflect that.

Creative Collaborators

Joint projects with photographers, filmmakers, writers, and researchers working at the intersection of the natural world and human responsibility.
